Why we do this
Romania does not lack digital systems. They exist, work on paper, and check all the boxes, but for real people they remain fragmented, cumbersome, and most often useless. Every day, millions of people lose time between institutions that don't communicate with each other, repeat the same information, and adapt to processes that were never designed for the user but for the system.

This is not a technology problem. It is a problem of vision and execution. Other countries have proven that a state can function simply, proactively, and invisibly, where services come to the citizen, not the other way around. Romania can do the same — not through strategies or promises, but through properly built products.

Cluj Hackathon 2026 is the place where we stop talking about how it should be and start building how it can be. In 48 hours, people from tech, business, and real-world fields come together to rethink the interaction between citizens and the state, with one single rule: everything must work, be simple, and be usable by anyone.

Digital Romania is not an abstract project. It is a responsibility. And change does not come from within the system, but from outside it — from those who choose to rebuild it.
